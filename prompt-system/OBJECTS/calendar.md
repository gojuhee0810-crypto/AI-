# Object Blueprint: Calendar (달력)

## OBJECT
Calendar

## CATEGORY
Finance (Primary Blue · Secondary Yellow · Accent Gray)

## PURPOSE
Instantly communicate a calendar / date at small icon sizes.

## CONSTRUCTION
A rounded rectangular calendar body. A solid color header band across the top of the body. Two small ring/loop shapes at the top edge, suggesting a hanging binding. A grid of small rounded-square date cells below the header (three columns, three rows). One accent-colored cell highlighted within the grid, representing today's date.

## SILHOUETTE
A rounded rectangle with a header band on top and a grid of date cells below, one cell clearly highlighted.

## PROPORTION
Calendar body ~70%. Date cell grid ~20%. Header band + highlighted cell ~10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The header band above a grid of date cells, with one cell highlighted in the accent color.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
None.

## AVOID
Readable numbers or dates, brand logo, realistic paper texture, spiral binding coil detail.
