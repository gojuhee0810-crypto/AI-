# Object Blueprint: Coupon (쿠폰)

## OBJECT
Coupon

## CATEGORY
Coupon (Primary Red #FF3B30 · Secondary Pink #FF6FA0 · Accent White #FFFFFF)

## PURPOSE
A paper ticket representing discount or promotional value in commerce transactions.

## CONSTRUCTION
A rectangular ticket with perforated or zigzag edges on both short sides. A star or gift symbol positioned in the upper left corner. Horizontal lines indicating text placeholder in the center area.

## SILHOUETTE
Horizontal rectangle with distinctive serrated edges on left and right ends, creating a ticket-like outline.

## PROPORTION
Main body 70%. Serrated edges 20%. Symbol and lines 10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
Zigzag or perforated vertical edges that suggest tear-off functionality.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
Dashed line through the middle suggesting where to cut, corner fold, percentage symbol (%).

## AVOID
Readable text or words, barcode, QR code, realistic paper texture, gradient shading, brand logos
