# Object Blueprint: Global Stock Fee (해외 주식 수수료)

## OBJECT
Global Stock Fee

## CATEGORY
Investment (Primary Green #2ECC71 · Secondary Mint #2ED9C3 · Accent Blue #2F6BFF)

## PURPOSE
Represents international stock trading fees and cross-border transaction costs.

## CONSTRUCTION
A stock certificate with upward arrow occupies center, globe icon positioned behind upper portion, single coin with currency symbol ($ or ₩) placed at corner edge.

## SILHOUETTE
Rectangular document with circular globe emerging from top, small coin accent at corner.

## PROPORTION
Main certificate body ~70%. Globe background ~20%. Coin accent ~10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
Globe pattern intersecting with stock chart arrow creates instant "international trading" signal.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
Subtle latitude/longitude grid lines on globe surface, minimalist bar chart silhouette on certificate.

## AVOID
Country flags, company logos, readable stock ticker symbols, price numbers, multiple currencies, realistic paper texture, gradient shading, glossy coin surface
