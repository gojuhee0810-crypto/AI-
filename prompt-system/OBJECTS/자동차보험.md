# Object Blueprint: Car Insurance (자동차보험)

## OBJECT
Car Insurance

## CATEGORY
Insurance (Primary Green #2ECC71 · Secondary Blue #2F6BFF · Accent White #FFFFFF)

## PURPOSE
An icon representing automobile insurance coverage and protection for vehicles.

## CONSTRUCTION
A car silhouette covered by a protective shield. The shield wraps around or overlays the car, symbolizing protection. A small medical cross or checkmark appears on the shield to indicate insurance coverage.

## SILHOUETTE
A compact car shape enclosed within or backed by a rounded shield form, readable at small sizes.

## PROPORTION
Main shield 70%. Car silhouette 20%. Cross/check symbol 10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The combination of car + shield instantly signals vehicle protection/insurance.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
Subtle horizontal lines on shield surface to suggest solidity.

## AVOID
Realistic car details (headlights, grilles, wheels), gradient reflections on shield, license plates, steering wheels, insurance policy text labels
