# Object Blueprint: Car (자동차)

## OBJECT
Car

## CATEGORY
Travel (Primary Blue · Secondary Yellow · Accent Orange)

## PURPOSE
Instantly communicate a car at small icon sizes.

## CONSTRUCTION
A low wide box-shaped car main body (not a single rounded blob) with a visible break line between the hood and the cabin. A single wide dark window band across the upper body (no door split, no window pillar). A bold thick stripe crossing the front bumper. Two round headlight shapes. Two large round wheels as the accent element group. A thin door-seam line.

## SILHOUETTE
The hood-to-cabin break line combined with the bold bumper stripe and the two large accent-colored wheels.

## PROPORTION
Main body ~70%. Window band + bumper stripe ~20%. Wheels ~10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The hood-to-cabin break line combined with the bold bumper stripe and the two large accent-colored wheels.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
None.

## AVOID
Two-part split windows, a window pillar, a door handle, a roof sign, a license plate, multiple cars, road or background scenery.
