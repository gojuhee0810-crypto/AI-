# Object Blueprint: Umbrella (우산)

## OBJECT
Umbrella

## CATEGORY
Insurance (Primary Green #2ECC71 · Secondary Blue #2F6BFF · Accent White #FFFFFF)

## PURPOSE
Protection symbol representing coverage and security against financial risks.

## CONSTRUCTION
Must have a curved canopy dome and a vertical handle. Should have visible panel segments radiating from center point.

## SILHOUETTE
Inverted bowl shape with straight vertical stem extending downward.

## PROPORTION
Main canopy 70%. Handle 20%. Accent tip/ferrule 10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
Curved dome divided into radiating triangular panels.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
None

## AVOID
Raindrops, realistic fabric texture, metal shaft detail, J-hook handle curve, folding mechanisms, stitching lines
