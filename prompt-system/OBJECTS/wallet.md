# Object Blueprint: Wallet

## OBJECT
Wallet

## CATEGORY
Payment (Primary Yellow · Secondary Gray · Accent Blue)

## PURPOSE
Instantly communicate a wallet used to hold cards and cash.

## CONSTRUCTION
A folded rectangular bifold wallet body with a visible card slot or fold seam line across the front. A card or bill edge peeking out of the top slot. A small stitching seam detail line near the edge.

## SILHOUETTE
A folded bifold silhouette with a card corner peeking out of the top slot.

## PROPORTION
Wallet body ~70-75%. Card/bill peeking out ~15-20%. Stitching detail ~10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The folded bifold silhouette with a card corner peeking out of the top slot.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
None — keep surfaces clean.

## AVOID
Realistic leather grain texture, zipper hardware, coin purse clasp, keychain or strap, brand logo.
