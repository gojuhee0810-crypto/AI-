# Object Blueprint: Smartphone (핸드폰)

## OBJECT
Smartphone

## CATEGORY
Payment (Primary Yellow · Secondary Gray · Accent Blue)

## PURPOSE
Instantly communicate a smartphone at small icon sizes.

## CONSTRUCTION
A rounded rectangular phone main body (portrait orientation). A large flat screen area filling most of the front face. A small round camera dot near the top edge of the screen. A thin bezel line around the screen edge.

## SILHOUETTE
The screen-filled rounded rectangle silhouette with a small top camera dot.

## PROPORTION
Phone body ~70-75%. Screen ~15-20%. Camera dot ~10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The screen-filled rounded rectangle silhouette with a small top camera dot.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
None.

## AVOID
Brand logo, a home button, any UI icons/app grid/clock/text inside the screen, multiple phones.
