# Object Blueprint: Point Pouch (복주머니)

## OBJECT
Point Pouch

## CATEGORY
Reward (Primary Yellow · Secondary Orange · Accent Red)

## PURPOSE
Represent accumulated reward points using the shape of a traditional Korean lucky drawstring pouch (복주머니).

## CONSTRUCTION
A round bag body. A cinched neck near the top, gathered and tied with a band, sitting below a small flared opening rim. A Won (₩) symbol centered on the round body. A small burst of 2-3 round point coins bursting upward out of the open rim at the top of the pouch, as if the points are spilling/popping out.

## SILHOUETTE
A round pouch body with a distinctly cinched, tied neck above it, and a small cluster of coins bursting out of the top opening.

## PROPORTION
Round body ~65%. Cinched neck + tie band ~15%. Bursting coin cluster ~15%. Won symbol ~5%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The cinched, tied neck sitting above a round pouch body, with points visibly bursting out of the open top.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
None — keep surfaces clean.

## AVOID
Realistic fabric texture, tassels or fringe, drawstring cords hanging loose, multiple pouches.
