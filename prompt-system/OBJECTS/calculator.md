# Object Blueprint: Calculator (계산기)

## OBJECT
Calculator

## CATEGORY
Finance (Primary Blue · Secondary Yellow · Accent Gray)

## PURPOSE
Instantly communicate a calculator at small icon sizes.

## CONSTRUCTION
A rounded rectangular calculator body (portrait orientation). A dark rectangular screen near the top of the body. A grid of small rounded square buttons below the screen (three columns, three rows). One tall accent button spanning two rows in the bottom-right column (representing the equals/enter key).

## SILHOUETTE
A rounded rectangle body with a dark screen strip and a grid of button dots below it.

## PROPORTION
Calculator body ~70%. Button grid ~20%. Screen + tall accent key ~10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The dark screen strip above a grid of rounded square buttons, with one tall button standing out in the bottom-right.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
None.

## AVOID
Readable numbers or symbols on the buttons, brand logo, realistic plastic texture.
