# Object Blueprint: Electric Bicycle (전기자전거)

## OBJECT
Electric Bicycle

## CATEGORY
Travel (Primary Blue #2F6BFF · Secondary Yellow #FFD400 · Accent Orange #FF9500)

## PURPOSE
Urban mobility vehicle with electric assistance for eco-friendly transportation.

## CONSTRUCTION
Frame consists of two wheels connected by curved body with handlebar at front. Battery pack integrated into frame between wheels. Pedals positioned at center lower section.

## SILHOUETTE
Streamlined bicycle profile with slightly thicker frame indicating electric components.

## PROPORTION
Main body and wheels 70%. Handlebar and battery pack 20%. Pedals and light 10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
Visible battery pack integrated into the frame's downtube.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
Small headlight, simplified gear mechanism indicator

## AVOID
Realistic spokes, chain details, brake cables, seat texture, tire treads, brand logos, text labels
