# Object Blueprint: Bank Passbook (통장)

> 2026-08-05: "입출금통장" 레퍼런스와 여러 라운드 비교해 확정 — 기둥 아이콘은 몸통과
> 확실히 다른 톤(고대비)으로, 페이지 스택은 크림톤으로, 코인은 통장보다 확실히 작은
> 보조 요소(2~3개 클러스터)로 렌더링해야 레퍼런스와 가깝다는 걸 실측으로 확인함.

## OBJECT
Bank Passbook

## CATEGORY
Finance (Primary Blue · Secondary Yellow · Accent Gray)

## PURPOSE
Instantly communicate a Korean bank passbook / account book at small icon sizes.

## CONSTRUCTION
A rounded rectangular booklet cover. A small bank/pillar building icon (triangular roof, three columns, base line) near the top of the cover, rendered in a clearly lighter, high-contrast neutral tone against the blue cover — never the same tone as the body. A light rounded rectangle label panel near the bottom of the cover. A thin stack of pages visible along the right edge of the booklet, rendered in a warm off-white/cream tone clearly distinct from the blue body. A small cluster of 2-3 round coins with an embossed Won (₩) symbol on the front coin, overlapping only the bottom-right corner of the booklet — clearly smaller than the booklet, a secondary accessory, not a dominant element.

## SILHOUETTE
A single rounded booklet shape with a small coin cluster peeking from behind its bottom-right corner.

## PROPORTION
Booklet cover ~70%. Page stack + coin cluster ~20%. Bank icon + label panel ~10%.

## RECOGNITION CUE
The high-contrast bank/pillar icon on the blue cover, paired with a small coin cluster at the bottom-right corner.

## OPTIONAL DETAILS
A thin self-tone spine line along the left edge of the booklet.
